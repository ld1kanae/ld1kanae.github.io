GMD-derived learning data in this directory is generated from the
Google Magenta Groove MIDI Dataset v1.0.0.

Dataset page:
https://magenta.tensorflow.org/datasets/groove

License:
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)

Citation:
Jon Gillick, Adam Roberts, Jesse Engel, Douglas Eck, and David Bamman.
"Learning to Groove with Inverse Sequence Transformations." ICML 2019.

This repository stores compact derived statistics/models, not a copy of the
original GMD MIDI/audio archive.
