This derived aggregate model uses the Groove MIDI Dataset v1.0.0
by Google LLC, licensed under CC BY 4.0.
Dataset: https://magenta.tensorflow.org/datasets/groove
Paper: Jon Gillick, Adam Roberts, Jesse Engel, Douglas Eck, and
David Bamman, "Learning to Groove with Inverse Sequence Transformations,"
ICML 2019.
This repository stores aggregate statistics only, not the source MIDI files.
